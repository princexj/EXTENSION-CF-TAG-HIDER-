# Privacy Policy for Codeforces Problem Tag Hider

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

**Codeforces Problem Tag Hider** ("the Extension") is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Usage

- **Zero Personal Data Collection**: The Extension does **NOT** collect, track, log, or monitor any personal information, browsing history, keystrokes, Codeforces credentials, or user activities.
- **Local Settings Storage**: The Extension uses `chrome.storage.sync` (or `chrome.storage.local`) solely to save your local UI preferences (such as your chosen display mode, whether to keep difficulty ratings visible, and whether to blur tags on the problemset page). This data remains strictly within your browser profile and synced across your personal browser sessions by Google's built-in browser sync services.

---

## 2. Network Requests & Third-Party Sharing

- **No Remote Servers**: The Extension has no backend servers or analytics platforms.
- **Zero Third-Party Sharing**: No user data, telemetry, or analytics are ever transmitted, sold, rented, or shared with any third parties or external services.

---

## 3. Permissions Justification

- **`storage`**: Used exclusively to remember your preferences (e.g. click-to-reveal mode vs. hover mode).
- **Host Permissions (`*://*.codeforces.com/*`, `*://*.codeforces.net/*`, `*://*.codeforc.es/*`)**: Required solely to inject the CSS stylesheet and content script on Codeforces problem and contest pages to hide algorithm tags. The extension does not run or access any other websites.

---

## 4. Changes to This Policy

If changes are made to this policy, the updated version will be posted directly in this repository.
